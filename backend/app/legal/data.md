# Your account and data

A short summary of what you can do with your data. The Privacy Policy has the details.

- **Where your data lives:** in this server's database and file store, inside your workspace.
  Other workspaces cannot see it; every request is checked on the server.
- **What leaves the server:** nothing by default. The optional AI assistant, AI candidates,
  datasheet reading, datasheet search, GPU runs and email each send only what the Privacy Policy
  lists, and only when the operator has configured them and you use that feature.
- **Your originals are never overwritten.** Every change to a project's inputs adds a new version;
  analyses, plans and exports are kept, and marked stale when the inputs they used change.
- **Export:** each project has exports with a hashed manifest (the change receipt).
- **Delete:** archive a project, delete workspace data on the workspace data page, or delete your
  account under Account → Delete account. Deletion is checked afterwards and the result is shown.
- **Accepted terms:** your account page shows which versions you accepted and when.
